Support

Questions, a bug, or a scoring rule that looks wrong? Email hello@leejustin.com and we'll get back to you. It helps to say which version you have (Settings shows it) and what you were doing.

Common questions

Do points mean money?
No. Points and faan in Faan Club are game points with no cash value. There is no betting and nothing to cash out.

Which rules does it use?
Hong Kong rules, with a choice between a table where any complete hand wins and one that needs 3 faan. House rules such as seven pairs and whether flowers count toward the minimum can be changed under Customize this table.

Where is my progress saved?
On your device. If you delete the app, your play history, saved match and photos of real-table wins are deleted with it.

How do I restore Faan Club Plus on a new phone?
Open Plus and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled in your iPhone's Settings under your name, then Subscriptions.

How do I turn off usage sharing?
In Settings, under Privacy. See the privacy policy for what it covers.
