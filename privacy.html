Faan Club.

Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Faan Club is made by Justin Lee ("we"). This policy explains what the Faan Club app does with information. The short version: the app works without an account, keeps your play on your device, shows no ads, and doesn't track you. Sharing anonymous usage data is optional and off unless you say yes.

What stays on your device

Your play history, saved match, lesson and practice progress, settings, coach reports and any photos you take or choose of wins at a real table are stored only on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them. (Tables you play with friends are the exception, described below.)

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a win at a real table. Choosing an existing photo uses your phone's own photo picker (Apple's on iPhone, Google's on Android), so the app only receives the one photo you pick and needs no access to the rest of your photos. The app never records sound.

Anonymous usage data (optional)

After your first hand, the app may ask whether you'd like to share anonymous usage data to help improve it. If you say yes, it sends events such as "a hand finished", "a lesson was completed", "a screen was viewed" or "the coach tip was opened", along with the app version and whether the device is iPhone or Android. Events are tied to a random ID created on your device, not to your name, email, Apple Account, Google Account or any device identifier. They don't include your tiles, your photos or anything you type.

This data is processed by PostHog, our analytics provider, on our behalf. We use it only to understand how the app is used and to improve it, never for advertising, and we don't sell it or share it with anyone else. PostHog doesn't keep your IP address, and we don't look up your location.

You can turn sharing on or off at any time in Settings, under Privacy. Turning it off stops sending and deletes the random ID from your device.

Purchases

Faan Club Plus is sold through the App Store on iPhone and through Google Play on Android, as a monthly or yearly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them, in your App Store account settings on iPhone or in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions on Android. Apple or Google handles payment; we never see your payment details.

To confirm what you've bought and restore it on a new device, the app checks your purchases through RevenueCat, our purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID and your purchase records (which plan and when), not your name or payment details.

Playing with friends

Play with friends is optional. When you host or join a table, the app talks to our rooms server, which runs on Cloudflare. It receives the display name you choose, the table's code and rules, the moves played, and a random player ID your phone creates. Your name is shown only to the other people at that table. The server deals the tiles and holds each hand, so nobody can see another player's tiles.

On iPhone, the random player ID is kept in your device's secure storage (the iOS Keychain), so it stays the same if you delete and reinstall the app.

On Android, the app keeps the player ID in its own encrypted storage, which Android removes when you uninstall the app. So that the ID also stays the same after a reinstall, a phone that doesn't have one yet makes it from the Android ID, an identifier Android gives each app on your phone. The Android ID that Faan Club sees is unique to Faan Club, so other apps can't use it to match you, and it changes if the phone is factory reset. The app scrambles it with a one-way hash to make the player ID, so the Android ID itself never leaves your phone and can't be worked out from the player ID. It isn't the advertising ID, which the app doesn't use.

The player ID isn't tied to your name, email, Apple Account or Google Account. We use it to count the free tables you've played this week and, through RevenueCat, to know whether you have Plus. It's also the random app user ID RevenueCat receives.

A table is deleted when it expires: 15 minutes after it's made if it never starts, a day after a match ends, and two days (a week with a Plus member) after everyone leaves a paused table. The record of which weeks you played free tables is deleted after 30 days.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of data.

Children

Faan Club isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects no personal information, a child using it isn't identified to us.

Your choices and rights

You can stop all data sharing in Settings at any time, and delete everything stored by the app by deleting the app, except the random player ID: on iPhone it stays in your device's Keychain until you erase the device, and on Android the same ID is made again if you reinstall the app, until the phone is factory reset. To leave a table with friends for good, tap the × beside it under Your tables. Because usage data isn't linked to you, we can't look it up by name; if you have a question or request, email us and we'll help where we can.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
