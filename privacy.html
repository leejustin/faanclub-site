Faan Club.

Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026

Faan Club is made by Justin Lee ("we"). This policy explains what the Faan Club app does with information. The short version: the app works without an account, keeps your play on your device, shows no ads, and doesn't track you. Sharing anonymous usage data is optional and off unless you say yes.

What stays on your device

Your play history, saved match, lesson and practice progress, settings, coach reports and any photos you take or choose of wins at a real table are stored only on your device. We can't see them. Deleting the app deletes them.

The camera is used only when you choose to photograph a win at a real table. Choosing an existing photo uses Apple's photo picker, so the app only receives the one photo you pick. The app never records sound.

Anonymous usage data (optional)

After your first hand, the app may ask whether you'd like to share anonymous usage data to help improve it. If you say yes, it sends events such as "a hand finished", "a lesson was completed", "a screen was viewed" or "the coach tip was opened", along with the app version and whether the device is iPhone or Android. Events are tied to a random ID created on your device, not to your name, email, Apple Account or any device identifier. They don't include your tiles, your photos or anything you type.

This data is processed by PostHog, our analytics provider, on our behalf. We use it only to understand how the app is used and to improve it, never for advertising, and we don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

You can turn sharing on or off at any time in Settings, under Privacy. Turning it off stops sending and deletes the random ID from your device.

Purchases

Faan Club Plus is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. To confirm what you've bought and restore it on a new device, the app checks your purchases with Apple. If we use a purchase-management service to do this, it receives a random app user ID and your purchase records, not your name or payment details.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of data.

Children

Faan Club isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Because the app collects no personal information, a child using it isn't identified to us.

Your choices and rights

You can stop all data sharing in Settings at any time, and delete everything stored by the app by deleting the app. Because usage data isn't linked to you, we can't look it up by name; if you have a question or request, email us and we'll help where we can.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
